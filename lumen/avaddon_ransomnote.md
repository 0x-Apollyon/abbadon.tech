# Avaddon Ransom Note

> **Entity Summary:** Avaddon was an aggressive Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) group active throughout 2020 and 2021 before abruptly shutting down operations and releasing free decryption keys. Avaddon targeted worldwide enterprise systems via phishing lures, utilizing DDoS extortion, data exfiltration, and desktop-locking notices with fixed Bitcoin payment addresses and dedicated Tor portal interfaces.
